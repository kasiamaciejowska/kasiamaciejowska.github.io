Privacy & cookies

Last updated: November 2026

Who is responsible

Kasia Maciejowska, Hypnotherapy Coaching (kasiamaciejowska.eu), is the controller of your personal data. You can reach me through the contact form.

What I collect and why

  • Contact form – name, email address and your message, to reply to you (legal basis: your consent, and steps taken at your request before an agreement). The form is sent via Web3Forms and protected by hCaptcha. Please do not include sensitive health details in the form.
  • Booking a call – done on Calendly, which processes your name, email address and chosen time (legal basis: preparing an agreement).
  • Analytics – Google Analytics (via Google Tag Manager) counts visits using cookies and anonymous usage data. It is only loaded if you accept (legal basis: your consent, art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and art. 11.7a Dutch Telecommunications Act).
  • Hosting – the hosting provider may log technical data such as your IP address.

Cookies

The only cookies are the Google Analytics ones, and only after you accept. Your choice is stored in your browser (local storage), not in a cookie. You can change it at any time via “Cookie settings” in the footer.

Retention and sharing

Messages and booking details are kept only as long as needed to handle your request and to meet legal obligations. Data is not sold. It is shared only with the services named above, which act as processors. Some of them (Google, Calendly, Web3Forms, the hosting provider) may process data outside the EEA, e.g. in the USA, based on safeguards such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction, deletion, restriction or transfer of your data, object to its use, and withdraw consent at any time. Contact me via the contact form. You also have the right to complain to the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens) or to the authority in your own EU country.
